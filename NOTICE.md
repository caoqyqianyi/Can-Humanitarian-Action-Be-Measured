# Notices

`LICENSE` covers the code only. Two things ship alongside it and are licensed
differently.

## Population series

The extract in `data/reference/` is the ISIMIP2a input dataset
`population_histsoc_national_annual` (HYDE methodology), served by the ISIMIP
data portal hosted by IIASA and published under **CC BY 4.0** (Attribution 4.0
International). Attribution to ISIMIP / IIASA and to the HYDE methodology is
required, and dataset rights should be re-checked at the portal before reuse.
The SHA-512 digest recorded in `data/reference/PROVENANCE.md` identifies the
exact version used here.

## Boundary geometry

`data/reference/ne_110m_admin0_world.json` is derived from Natural Earth
(naturalearthdata.com), which is in the public domain: no attribution is
required, though it is good practice and is given here. It is a cartographic
layer, not an authoritative boundary representation.
