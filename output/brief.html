08How this brief was produced

Four stages, no manual step in the middle. The population series behind sections four and five is fetched, checksummed and cached by the first stage; everything after it reads from disk.

STAGE 01

Evidence, declared

Custodian, access condition and licence are written into sources.yml before a single indicator is matched. The download records its own provenance and digest.

→
STAGE 02

Registry, as data

Each known indicator carries its unit, cadence, promised disaggregations and disclosure tier in YAML — a file CI validates, not a paragraph in a guideline.

→
STAGE 03

Match, then five checks

Token and phrase matching with a 0.45 floor; then availability, disaggregation, cadence, geography and baseline, and disclosure. A weak match is refused, never guessed.

→
STAGE 04

Verdict, per line

Monitorable as written, reportable with one named caveat, or no data trail. The top cause is printed so the fix is obvious.

This pagebrief.html — one file, no network, prints complete
Scorecardscorecard.csv — one row per indicator, for a reviewer
Reportingpowerbi/ — star schema and five DAX measures

The disclosure gate runs before any of the three is written: an internal source stays aggregate, and cell counts below five are generalised.

Limitations. The audit tests whether data exists and is usable; it does not test whether an indicator measures what its result statement claims. Gridded hazard layers (flood, crop) are catalogued and licensed but need an administrative boundary raster before they become national indicators.
